## Product Vision — read before building

The authoritative product vision for Druppr lives in [`docs/VISION.md`](docs/VISION.md). It is the single source of truth for what this company is building over the next 5–10 years.

**Every future UX, UI, engineering, and architecture decision must remain aligned with `docs/VISION.md`.** Before designing a screen, scoping a feature, or writing code, confirm the direction is consistent with the mission, product philosophy, trust philosophy, and decision framework defined there. When the current implementation and the vision conflict, the vision describes intent — build toward it, and distinguish "Current State" from "Target State" as that document does.

Do not restate the vision here; read it there. This file covers *how* we work in this repo; `docs/VISION.md` covers *what* and *why* we are building.
